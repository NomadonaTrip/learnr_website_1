## FAQ

**Q: How is LearnR different from other adaptive learning apps?**
A: Most adaptive apps do one thing—adjust difficulty OR track concepts. LearnR combines four algorithms: knowledge tracing for breadth, item response theory for depth, knowledge graphs for concept connections, and spaced repetition for retention. It's not a quiz app with adaptive features. It's a complete system.

**Q: How does LearnR know what reading content I need?**
A: When the adaptive engine identifies a knowledge gap, it automatically adds relevant reading material to your personal library. You can study it whenever you're ready—your practice session continues uninterrupted. We track what you've read so your learning stays complete.

**Q: Will LearnR cover everything on my exam?**
A: Yes. LearnR maps every concept associated with your exam and guides you through complete coverage. Your personalized path ensures nothing gets missed.

**Q: How does LearnR help me remember what I've learned?**
A: Spaced repetition. The system schedules concept reviews at scientifically-optimal intervals—just before you'd forget. This transforms cramming into lasting knowledge.

**Q: How many questions does the initial assessment take?**
A: Typically 15-25 questions. The adaptive engine gathers more information per question than traditional apps, so it needs far fewer to map your knowledge state accurately.

**Q: Which certification exams does LearnR support?**
